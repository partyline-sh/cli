# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Disagree about the work, not the person.

## What that means here

This project runs on direct technical argument — "that will break because X" is welcome and useful,
and softening it into vagueness helps nobody. What is not welcome is aiming that directness at a
person rather than at their reasoning.

Concretely, and not exhaustively:

- Critique the code, the design, or the claim. Not the author's competence or motives.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and unwelcome attention are grounds for removal. So is persistently
  relitigating a settled decision to wear people down.
- Assume good faith on the first misreading. Most friction on the internet is tone in text, not
  malice.
- If you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody is keeping score.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space, and to conduct
elsewhere that materially affects someone's ability to participate here.

## Reporting

Email **me@darcyreno.com**. Reports are handled privately.

This is a small project, so there is no committee — the maintainer decides, and will say what was
decided and why. Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban, proportionate to what
happened and whether it continued after being raised.

If your report concerns the maintainer, say so; it will be handled by referring it to someone
uninvolved rather than by the subject of the report.
